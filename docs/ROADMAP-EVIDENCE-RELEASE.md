# Independent M1–M8 release audit

Reviewed 14 October 2026 against `ROADMAP.md`, `ROADMAP-SCOPE.json`, the milestone evidence files and actual story/data/renderer files. This is an additional bounded review, not a replacement for each chapter's full scene review or the coordinator's final shared regression.

## Findings sent to the coordinator

- **M8 acceptance gap:** the frozen fallback requires an empire outline and selected sourced territories alongside the cleared patchwork overlay (`ROADMAP.md`, Source fallbacks). The first reviewed `hre-patchwork` implementation had the dated Reilly overlay and institutional city locators but no selected territorial or empire-outline representation. The coordinator confirmed the requirement and assigned the remaining geometry work; no scope reduction was accepted. Recheck the resulting geometry, dates, provenance and both viewports before accepting M8.
- **M4 source conflict:** `us-expansion` initially dated its St Charles expedition waypoint to 14 May 1804. The [NPS chronological index](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/travellewisandclark/expedition-by-date.htm) gives this date, but its [linked St Charles account](https://www.nps.gov/places/st-charles-historic-district.htm) quotes Clark's diary recording arrival on 16 May. The same detailed page has a contradictory May 14 summary. Recommend using the directly quoted May 16 arrival, acknowledging the summary conflict, and updating builder, waypoint, timeline and evidence together. The other three selected waypoint dates agree with the cited chronological index.
- **M1 documentation mismatch, corrected by coordinator:** roadmap prose previously called `countryside` a fishing-fleet substitute. It now describes the actual rural/urban net-migration comparison, without changing the frozen story ID or reducing a required gate.

## Independent checks and observations

- Read M1–M8 source/representation reports and inspected selected actual scenes/datasets for Turkey, Rome, USA, EU, Ukraine, WWII and HRE. Selected quantitative metadata are explicitly bounded: EU budget EUR million and named financing arithmetic; EU regional GDP/head PPS, EU27=100, one 2023 publication; Ukraine annual stocks versus the separate year-end operational estimate; NASA display imagery without radiance or territorial inference; CShapes diplomatic observations rather than occupation surfaces.
- Read the [Met's Ottonian Art account](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/ottonian-art) independently: Aachen royal coronation 936, imperial investiture 962 and the northern/Italian geographic context support the inspected HRE scenes. Read [UNESCO Ravenna](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/788/) independently; its detailed statement supports the AD 402 imperial seat used in `rome-split`.
- Inspected full-size desktop/mobile HRE patchwork close views and the desktop WWII western atlas overlay. The historical-map layers are present and their approximate alignment and dates are described visibly; the finer labels remain archival scan text rather than promised survey-quality geometry. WWII arrows are identified as a multi-date campaign summary, not a simultaneous front.
- Inspected the Holocaust story/layer configuration: static muted event cards, plain-language context, sourced overall victim total, no decorative victim scaling or fabricated deportation routes. Inspected Ukraine front/snapshot/refugee scene dates and explicit coverage breaks. Monthly UCDP event layers use monthly filtering; their meaning is events, not deaths or control.
- Reviewed shared engine source for dataset-local caches, annual/full-date validity, category regions, event-kind filtering, image raster handling and timeline controls. Historical/grid/overlay caches now key by the declared data path rather than reusable local aliases. No additional concrete shared-engine failure was identified in this read-only sample. Coordinator-owned browser fixtures/global regression provide execution evidence.
